# Nova Protocol

Build a space mission from scratch, play every step of it, and discover what it takes to reach another world.

**[Play it on itch.io](https://jimbogate47.itch.io/nova-protocol)** · Free, in your browser, no install needed.

## What is it?

Nova Protocol is a space management and engineering game for one or two players. You lead a single expedition made of connected phases, and every choice you make carries into the next one: the materials you gather decide what you can build, and what you build decides how far you get and what you can discover.

## How it plays

**1. Gather.** Drive a vehicle with a limited battery through a maze to collect metals, fuel and crystals. Stopping at the right moment multiplies your reward.

**2. Smelt.** In the foundry, minerals arrive against the clock. Recognize each one and type its name before it is lost, because every mineral you waste is resources you will not get back.

**3. Build.** Turn what you smelted into the ship: pick its parts, load the instruments you will need, and respect the weight your engine can carry.

**4. Cross space.** Two players share the screen with different jobs. One manages the orbit and the fuel, steering the ship toward the moon. The other pilots through a field of meteorites and solar winds, and can phase out to slip through danger. Neither can win alone, so coordination is the game.

**5. Analyze.** Once in orbit, use the instruments you built to study the planet. You cannot test everything, so you choose which patterns to compare, spend your limited analyses carefully, and decide when something is simply not there. A final report grades your mission.

## What you learn

- **Value is in processing.** Raw materials become valuable when they are refined and turned into technology.
- **Science drives the design.** The goals of a mission decide what the ship carries, not the other way around.
- **Every step depends on the last.** Resources, weight, fuel and time are limited, so planning matters.
- **The ground team matters.** Engineers, chemists and programmers make a mission possible long before liftoff.
- **Teamwork and decisions under uncertainty.** You coordinate with another person and learn from your mistakes by trying again.

Each stage is short and easy to repeat, so you learn the whole process by playing it over and over.

## Why we made it

To inspire young people to explore science and technology through play, to raise awareness of why transforming natural resources matters, and to show that anyone can be part of the team behind a space mission.
